Forever & Always, My Rudra 🌹😭

My dearest Rudra,

If you have reached this final screen, I hope you felt every drop of devotion, thought, and love that went into making this for you. But beyond the games and the code, I need you to understand the real truth behind all of this: you are the most precious person in my entire existence.

I look at you and I honestly wonder what good I ever did in this world to deserve someone as pure, beautiful, and loving as you. You came into my life like a quiet prayer I didn't even realize I was making. Before you, my heart was just beating; with you, it is finally living. You healed places in me you didn't break, and you loved me at times when I didn't even know how to love myself.

I know life isn't always easy. Distance, timing, and everyday struggles get heavy. But I want to make a solemn promise to you right now, Rudra: no matter how hard things get, no matter how distant we may feel, or how much noise the world creates around us, I will always, always choose you. Every single day. Without hesitation. Without a second thought.

When you are happy, I want to be the one celebrating with you. When you are sad, overwhelmed, or tired, I want to be the shoulder you rest your head on and the voice that reminds you how strong and loved you are. You never have to face this world alone ever again, because my heart is forever tied to yours.

I miss you in ways I cannot put into sentences. I miss the sound of your laugh, the warmth of your touch, the way your eyes look when you talk about things you love, and the quiet comfort of just being near you. If I could pull down the stars and give them to you just to see you smile for one second, I would do it without thinking twice.

Thank you for choosing me. Thank you for staying. Thank you for giving me a love so real and deep that it changed me forever. You are my home, my peace, my dream, and my entire world.

I love you more than words, code, or time could ever express, Rudra.

Yours completely & endlessly,
— Sree ❤️
